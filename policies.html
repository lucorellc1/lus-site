Shipping

  • All cards are personalized and made to order.

Refunds

Because every order is personalized and made to order, all sales are final and we do not offer refunds.

Card designs are selected at random and come in different rarities. Specific designs or rarities can't be chosen or guaranteed, and receiving a particular design or rarity is not grounds for a refund or exchange.

Terms of sale

  • Product. Each person's order includes two personalized metal engraved collectible cards, in standard playing-card size (about 2.5 × 3.5 inches).
  • Random selection. Designs are randomly selected from our current designs, which vary in rarity.
  • Pricing. Prices are listed per person in US dollars. Group pricing is offered for orders placed together; each person may pay separately. Prices and promotions may change without notice, and discount codes are valid only while active.
  • Payment. Payments are processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store your card details.
  • Collectibles. Cards are sold as collectible art and are not redeemable for cash or any other value.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to fulfill your order: your name, email address, shipping address, and any username you provide at checkout. Payment information is handled by our payment processor and never reaches us.

We use this information to make and ship your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell your personal information. To ask about or delete your information, email lucorellc1@gmail.com.
