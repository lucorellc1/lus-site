FAQ

What do I get?

Two personalized, metal engraved collectible cards per person, in standard playing-card size.

Can I choose which designs I get?

No. Designs are selected at random and come in different rarities, so specific designs or rarities can't be chosen or guaranteed.

How long does delivery take?

Cards are made to order and typically arrive within 2–3 weeks of your order.

Can I get a refund?

Because every card is personalized and made to order, all sales are final. See our refund policy.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by our payment processor. Your card details are never stored on this site.
